What's in it

  • A summary for your preparer: who's filing, what's here, what's still to come, and what needs a look.
  • Your {{ year }} documents, sorted into folders by type (W-2s, 1099s, K-1s, …).
  • Your estimated tax payments and your donations, as lists.
  • Account numbers show only their last four digits, in the summary and the file names.

The .zip isn't locked. Unlike your binder and its backups, it isn't encrypted: anyone who has the file can open your documents. TaxBinder never sends it anywhere. Give it to your preparer through their secure portal, then delete it from Downloads.
